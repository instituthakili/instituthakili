resume_en: The paper examines how Mali's mining and petroleum codes deal with technology transfer. It highlights the inadequacy of the substantive framework, which makes no specific reference to the notion while promoting local content to some extent, then the need to adapt it to the challenges of the sector, and makes recommendations to that end.
